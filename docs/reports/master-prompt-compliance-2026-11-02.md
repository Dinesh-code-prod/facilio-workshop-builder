## Not done, and why
1. Browser push and timed Teams reminders: the platform provides neither a push service nor a scheduler.
2. Reading pictures inside the app: depends on the platform accepting picture input for the vision model; use Admin > AI > Check image reading after the next deploy, and Use My Claude until it passes.
3. PDF reading in Add from Claude, forms / layouts / tabs / roles in the export, an approved-example store, document indexing in search, image uploads for Discover entries, notification preferences.
