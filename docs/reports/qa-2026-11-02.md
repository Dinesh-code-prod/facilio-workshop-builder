## What passed
- **Load, 100 people at once:** 300 ordinary requests median 145 ms, 95th percentile 243 ms, 0 failed; 100 AI drafts at once all succeeded; 40 of 40 cross-project reads refused; one person bursting 30 AI requests gets a clear "wait 20 seconds" and the retry works.
- **Stress run (QA-01 fixed):** 100 projects created; 5,000 notes and links saved; 3 MB documents split under the 128 KB limit and read back whole; a document over 5 MB refused with a clear message; two saves of the same version give a conflict, not an overwrite; a 40 MB upload starts in parts; an `.exe` and a 400 MB file are refused; 300 connector calls succeeded with 30 of 30 cross-project reads refused; opening a heavy project median 117 ms. Largest stored value 40 KB of 128 KB. 56,845 database calls in total.
- **Security:** 17 of 17 browser security checks; no secrets in the repository; no known vulnerabilities in production packages; every image has alt text; keyboard Tab and skip link work.
- **Passing browser checks:** owner hand-over 7/7, tasks 40/40, task board 15/15, guide drafts 17/17, configuration diagram 21/21, AI parts 7/7, flow motion 10/10, BRD figure size 12/12, whiteboard editing 20/20, whiteboard hover delete 27/27, BRD Word editing 11/11, whiteboard align 12/12, workshop hubs 7/7, Claude hand-off 7/7, project settings 4/4, privacy and keyboard 8/8, session persistence 9/9.

## Storage and capacity
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is any kind of data not stored? | Every record kind and document section was written and read back in the stress run. Records over about 100 KB are gzipped and split, so size is limited by the 5 MB document cap, not by the 128 KB database value |
| Biggest file | 100 MB per upload for BRD exports and configuration packages; 30 to 60 MB for MSA and dashboards; uploaded in 16 MB parts |
| What happens at the limits | A document over 5 MB, a file over its category limit, an unsupported type and an AI burst each return a clear message |
| 100 projects, 100 people, AI at once | Median 145 ms; AI limited to 20 requests per minute per person by the platform (40 per 10 minutes by the app) |
| Where it lives | Platform database `ctx.db` and private file store `ctx.files`; layout in docs/05-backend-schema.md |

## What was not tested
Live facilio.work load (tests use a stand-in platform); real mail; Teams; customer sign-in; a real Claude with the connector from an organisation account.
