# UI audit, 12 Nov 2026: is the Apple look everywhere?

The reference is the Apple look in `src/ui.css`, as described in CLAUDE.md under Look and feel: system font, weights 400/500/600, capsule buttons, 12 px fields, 20 px cards, no gradients, no glow, no uppercase and no violet.

## How it was checked
- `tests/ui/audit.mjs` (Playwright, Edge) opened every screen in a real browser. That covers sign-in, the projects home, the account menu, all Admin sections, every project menu page with its tabs (up to 8 per page), and the first create or edit dialog on each page. Each screen was opened at 1440, 1280, 768 and 390 px wide, which makes **360 screens** in each pass. There were three passes: before the fixes, after the fixes, and a final one.
- The new `tests/ui/design-checks.js` runs next to the geometry checks in `tests/ui/checks.js`. It reads the computed style of every visible element against the rules above.
- I followed up in the browser pane on the tablet top bar, the Guide me card on a phone, the Mockup cards on a phone, and the Product tour tiles.
- `npm test` passes and `node build.mjs` succeeds. The audit passes found no page errors.

## Kept on purpose (owner rules, not leftovers)
- The dark blue gradient page headers and their soft navy shadow ("leave the dark blue gradient page headers as they are").
- The night-city banner and its buttons on Customer projects, the sign-in art, the intro, Discover more, the What's new hero and the Product tour hero (radial glow, serif titles).
- The Lifecycle timeline grid lines: they are a repeating gradient that draws the day grid.
- Focus rings (a 4 px blue ring on fields and the current step) and the Guide me spotlight.

## Findings that are not real problems
- The "button shape" and "off scale" findings came from `.btn.link` text links. They are capsule buttons now (see the table).
- About 300 "tiny text" findings on tablet and phone come from icon-only buttons whose label is kept for screen readers.
- The `.tl-search` inputs report a radius of 0 because the rounded box is their wrapper.

## Still open (could not be fixed here, or needs a decision)
- `node tests/ui/css-legacy-audit.mjs` still counts 166 gradients and 232 large radii in the older style files. Most are overridden by `src/ui.css` at run time and the browser audit no longer sees them. Deleting the dead rules is a separate clean-up with `tools/css-prune.mjs`.
- The projects home and sign-in pages still carry the older art. CLAUDE.md records this as not done.
- On a 768 px tablet the tab row on Project introduction pushes "Import a reference" partly out of view, inside its own scroll area.
- The sign-in rail buttons (`.lsn-rail`) have no visible label at tablet width.

## Not checked
- Dark mode (the app has none) and high-contrast mode.
- Every dropdown, menu and dialog on every page. Only the first create or edit dialog per page, the account menus, and the dialogs above were opened.
- Keyboard-only walk-through.
- The live site (it needs the owner's sign-in): this was run on the local dev server with the demo data.
